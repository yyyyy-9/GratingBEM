# 相关参考文献

以下文献涉及周期光栅、边界积分方程、准周期 Green 函数、多入射散射和相关数值方法，按主题列示。

## 光栅边界积分方法

1. A. Rathsfeld, G. Schmidt, B. H. Kleemann (2006). **On a Fast Integral Equation Method for Diffraction Gratings.** *Communications in Computational Physics*, 1(6), 984–1009. [期刊 DOI](https://doi.org/10.4208/cicp.2006.v1.p984) · [WIAS 书目与全文入口](https://wias-berlin.de/publications/wias-publ/run.jsp?number=1090&template=abstract&type=Preprint&year=2006).

2. Y. Wu, Y. Y. Lu (2009). **Analyzing diffraction gratings by a boundary integral equation Neumann-to-Dirichlet map method.** *Journal of the Optical Society of America A*, 26(11), 2444–2451. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.26.002444) · [作者预印本](https://personal.cityu.edu.hk/mayylu/papers/yumao3.pdf).

## 传输积分方程与角点

3. R. E. Kleinman, P. A. Martin (1988). **On Single Integral Equations for the Transmission Problem of Acoustics.** *SIAM Journal on Applied Mathematics*, 48(2), 307–325. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1137/0148016).

4. J. Helsing, A. Karlsson (2018). **On a Helmholtz transmission problem in planar domains with corners.** [作者预印本 arXiv:1711.09796](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.09796) · [期刊 DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcp.2018.05.044).

## 周期 Green 函数与 Ewald 方法

5. C. M. Linton (1998). **The Green’s Function for the Two-Dimensional Helmholtz Equation in Periodic Domains.** *Journal of Engineering Mathematics*, 33, 377–401. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1004377501747).

6. F. Capolino, D. R. Wilton, W. A. Johnson (2005). **Efficient Computation of the 2-D Green’s Function for 1-D Periodic Structures Using the Ewald Method.** *IEEE Transactions on Antennas and Propagation*, 53(9), 2977–2984. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/TAP.2005.854556) · [作者机构全文入口](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/6vd3x1c2).

## 多入射角与周期散射求解

7. A. Gillman, A. Barnett (2013). **A fast direct solver for quasi-periodic scattering problems.** *Journal of Computational Physics*, 248, 309–322. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcp.2013.04.015) · [作者预印本](https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.1247).

8. M. H. Cho, A. H. Barnett (2015). **Robust fast direct integral equation solver for quasi-periodic scattering problems with a large number of layers.** *Optics Express*, 23(2), 1775–1799. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.23.001775) · [作者预印本](https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.5003).

9. S. P. Groth, D. P. Hewett, S. Langdon (2018). **A hybrid numerical-asymptotic boundary element method for high frequency scattering by penetrable convex polygons.** *Wave Motion*, 78, 32–53. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.wavemoti.2017.12.008) · [作者预印本](https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.07745).
